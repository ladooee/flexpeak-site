Privacy Policy for FlexPeak

Effective Date: April 29, 2026

FlexPeak respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how FlexPeak handles information when you use the app.

FlexPeak is designed to help PG&E Hourly Flex Pricing pilot customers view hourly energy prices, calculate EV charging costs, and analyze usage and cost information.

Information You Enter in the App

FlexPeak may allow you to enter app settings such as your electricity provider, PG&E rate plan, circuit ID, price thresholds, price offsets, vehicle information, charging preferences, and usage-related information.

This information is used to provide app features such as price tracking, EV charging calculations, usage analysis, and widgets.

Data Storage

FlexPeak stores app settings and app data on your device unless otherwise stated in the app.

Support

If you contact us for support, we may receive your email address and any information you include in your message. We use this information only to respond to your request and provide support.

Analytics and Third-Party Services

FlexPeak does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services unless disclosed here.

Data Sharing

FlexPeak does not sell your personal information. We do not share your personal information with third parties except as required by law or as necessary to provide support.

Data Retention

App data stored on your device remains on your device unless you delete it or remove the app. Support emails may be retained as needed to respond to requests and maintain records.

Children’s Privacy

FlexPeak is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: support@getflexpeak.com
