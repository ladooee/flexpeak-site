FlexPeak

Track PG&E Hourly Flex Pricing and make smarter energy decisions during the Flex Pricing Pilot Program.

Built for PG&E Flex Pricing Pilot customers

FlexPeak helps PG&E customers participating in the Hourly Flex Pricing pilot program understand hourly electricity prices, plan usage around lower-cost periods, and monitor how their energy choices affect their bill.

Features

Hourly price chart

View hourly flex prices throughout the day so you can quickly spot high-cost and low-cost periods.

EV charging calculator

Estimate the best time to charge your electric vehicle based on your battery level, target charge, charge rate, and pricing strategy.

Usage analysis

Analyze usage and cost over time to better understand how you are performing on the pilot program.

Custom settings

Configure your provider, PG&E rate plan, circuit ID, price offset, and price thresholds.

Widgets

Keep track of the current energy price from your Home Screen or Lock Screen.

Dark mode

FlexPeak supports light and dark appearances for comfortable use throughout the day.
